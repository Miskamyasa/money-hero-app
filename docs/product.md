# Product

## Vision

Money Hero mobile is a lightweight portfolio tracker for people who manually maintain their holdings and want a fast, honest view of portfolio value and market movement.

The app should feel like the desktop product made pocket-sized: same core mental model, mobile-native layout, less operational drag.

## Target Users

- Existing Money Hero desktop users.
- Users who track stocks, indexes, commodities, and currencies manually.
- Users who prefer local portfolio tracking over account-heavy finance apps.

## Solution Overview

The app shows a portfolio dashboard with balance, market widgets, currency rates, expected balance, and fetch progress. Users can customize currencies and ticker lists, then enter holding amounts and weights so the app can calculate portfolio value from Yahoo Finance prices.

The product should default to useful market context, but let users remove default widgets and shape the tracker around their own portfolio.

## Product Boundaries

- Money Hero tracks and summarizes portfolio data.
- Money Hero does not place trades.
- Money Hero does not connect to brokerages.
- Money Hero does not require an account for the initial mobile direction.
- Money Hero does not provide financial advice.
